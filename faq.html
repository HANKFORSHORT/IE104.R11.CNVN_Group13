Astra currently supports the most popular competitive programming languages on our judge servers:

  • C++ 17 and C++ 20 (g++ -O2)
  • Python 3.11 and PyPy 3

  • Rust 1.75

We use an Elo-based rating system tailored for multi-participant contests. After each rated round, your rating updates according to your actual standing compared to your expected seed.

New accounts start at 1200 and typically stabilize after 3 to 5 rated contests. Visit the Guide for a full breakdown of ranks and rating formulas.

If you suspect a broken test case, weak checker, or inaccurate problem statement, please head over to our Report an issue page and select Wrong test case / verdict.

Include the problem code (for example, 2264F), your submission ID, and the counterexample input so our problem setters can verify and rejudge within 48 hours.

Yes — we welcome original problem proposals from the community! Use the Report an issue form and select Propose a problem, or reach out via our Contact page with:

  • A clear problem statement and input/output constraints
  • At least 3 sample test cases with explanations
  • Your model solution and intended time/memory complexity

Our coordinator team reviews new proposals every week and will follow up with you by email.
