About the platform

A calm place to train algorithmic thinking.

Astra is an online competitive programming platform featuring a curated problemset, timed rated contests, a practice gym, and transparent ratings that track real algorithmic progress.

Developed as part of the IE104 — Internet and Web Technologies course, our mission is to deliver a distraction-free, responsive environment with full light and dark mode support.
